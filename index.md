# Hanuman Chalisa Privacy Policy

Publisher: Kasi Yaswanth

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Hanuman Chalisa stores your selected languages, appearance, reading position, bookmarks, learned verses and practice records locally on your Android device. This app does not create accounts, collect personal details, display advertisements, use analytics or transmit your practice data. It requests no internet permission.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled through the app manifest and extraction rules. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local progress. Reset progress in Settings removes study records and positions while retaining language and appearance preferences. Device manufacturers may have separate system-level backup behavior outside this app’s control.

All verse and meaning audio is bundled in the app and plays locally. No text or recordings are sent to Azure or another service by the installed app. No external audio or fonts are downloaded. Optional reference links open in your external browser, where the linked website's privacy policy applies. There are no in-app purchases in this build. The app does not provide medical services; devotional passages are religious text and study material.
